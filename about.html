Origin story

My background in advertising and visual design shaped how I work today. It taught me that details matter, but I'm much more interested in how the whole system works and how products grow over time.

SCENE 02● 00:24:13

My thing

I enjoy stepping in when things aren't fully defined yet: taking messy requirements or complex ideas and turning them into clear flows and structures that make sense for users and for the business.

SCENE 03● 00:51:40

Design is a team sport

I work closely with product and engineering to find the best solutions together. I think about the long-term impact of our decisions, so we build something scalable, not just a quick fix.

AI is part of how I work: I use it to prototype, explore more directions faster and ship real things, like this site. The judgment, the taste and the final call stay human.

SCENE 04● 01:13:08

After hours

I use Blender to explore spatial concepts, which shapes how I think about immersive interfaces. I'm curious about where XR and AI are taking product experiences, and how design systems need to evolve to support them.

Also: a lot of horror movies.
